Dispatcher event log
    AI agent model and environment classification

    State: vehicle position, availability, current mission, emergency queue with priorities, road conditions and time.

    Actions: assign a compatible unit, traverse a directed road, wait, pause, reroute, cancel and complete a mission. The goal is arrival at the assigned emergency destination.

    Performance: priority/FIFO assignment, minimum compatible ETA, wait time, travel distance/time, completed missions and replans. Dijkstra and A* optimize the selected cost; BFS minimizes hops and DFS finds a first path.

    Environment: fully observable simulated state; sequential and dynamic; deterministic route planning on each snapshot, stochastic traffic with a reproducible seed; cooperative multi-agent fleet with shared hazards; discrete road graph plus continuous travel interpolation. Pause freezes travel, while traffic has its own controls.

    PEAS: performance = the metrics above; environment = roads, units, emergencies and incidents; actuators = movement, assignment and rerouting; sensors = known graph, incident registry and fleet positions. No live traffic feed or real emergency driving guarantees.

    Vehicle profiles simulate different speeds and road-class delays.

    Blocked roads are excluded. Minimum distance uses physical road length; other objectives include incident costs. BFS/DFS do not optimize weighted objectives.
